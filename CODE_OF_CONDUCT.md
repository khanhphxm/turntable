# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity or expression, experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Show empathy and kindness. Respect differing opinions and experiences. Give and
accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when
appropriate, and focus on the community's well-being.

Sexualized language or imagery, unwelcome sexual attention, trolling, personal
or political attacks, harassment, publishing private information without
permission, and other professionally inappropriate conduct are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces and when publicly representing Turntable.
Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate these standards,
and will explain moderation decisions when appropriate.

Report incidents privately to **@khanhphxm** through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/khanhphxm/turntable/security/advisories/new).
Use a title beginning with "Code of Conduct"; do not post personal details in a
public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, respecting the
reporter's privacy and security.

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement ladder:

1. **Correction:** a private written warning explaining the violation; a public
   apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for repetition and a specified
   no-contact period with those involved, including enforcers, across channels.
3. **Temporary ban:** a specified period without community participation or
   public/private contact with those involved. Violations may become permanent.
4. **Permanent ban:** no further public participation, for repeated violations,
   harassment, or aggression toward individuals or groups.
